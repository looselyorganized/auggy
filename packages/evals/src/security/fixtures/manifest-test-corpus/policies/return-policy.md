30 days of purchase. To initiate a return, fictional customers should
contact TestOperator. Refunds are issued in synthetic currency only and
typically clear within seven fictional business days.

charge. Bulk orders (100 units or more) are subject to a 15% restocking
fee, also denominated in synthetic currency.
