prices, and specifications below are synthetic — no real transactions are
supported.

A baseline synthetic widget machined from imaginary alloy. Priced at $42
per unit. Targeted at fictional hobbyists and entry-level synthetic
metallurgy classrooms.

## The Premium Gizmo

An upgraded widget variant finished with a quantum-polish surface
treatment. Priced at $128 per unit. Ships with a fictional certificate of
authenticity.

a synthetic instructor's guide. Priced at $295 per unit. Designed for
fictional training facilities.
